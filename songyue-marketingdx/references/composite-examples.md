# Composite Examples

These are public-safe composite examples abstracted from repeated marketing situations. They are not real customer cases and must not be described as such.

## Integrated Marketing

A regional beverage brand proposes a summer campaign: short videos about office fatigue, convenience-store displays, influencer tasting notes, and a coupon mini-program. The plan says the goal is “make young people feel refreshed,” but does not decide whether the business problem is trial, repeat purchase, price perception, or channel penetration.

Useful diagnosis pattern: route as `整合营销`; evaluate all six dimensions; push the plan to define the commercial priority before judging channels.

## Communications/PR

A B2B productivity tool plans a launch PR wave around “new work efficiency.” It includes a press release, founder interview, analyst briefing, and social posts, but lacks a clear contradiction, news hook, or proof point that media would repeat.

Useful diagnosis pattern: route as `传播/公关`; focus on public topic, credibility, evidence, and sequence; mark pure creative execution as `本方案不评` unless copy is supplied.

## Strategy/Brand

A mid-priced home appliance brand wants to reposition from “durable” to “daily comfort partner.” The document lists market trends and competitor slogans but does not explain which audience tension the brand can credibly own.

Useful diagnosis pattern: route as `策略/品牌`; focus on problem definition, audience scene, proposition, and evidence; mark channel rollout and creative craft as `本方案不评`.

## Creative/Content

A skincare content team proposes a set of short videos dramatizing awkward self-care moments. The scripts have character detail and jokes, but the brand role appears only as a final product shot, and the audience tension is broad.

Useful diagnosis pattern: route as `创意/内容`; focus on audience scene, proposition-to-idea connection, memorability, and evidence; mark business definition and channel design as `本方案不评` unless supplied.
