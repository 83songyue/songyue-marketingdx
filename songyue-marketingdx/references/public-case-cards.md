# Public Composite Case Cards

These case cards are public-safe composite cases. They are abstracted and recombined from repeated marketing situations. They are not real client cases, do not contain original client wording, and must not be presented as factual project records.

## 1. Old Platform Wants To Become Fashionable

Type: `策略/品牌`

Scenario: A practical mass platform launches a lifestyle sub-brand and wants users to believe it is fashionable.

Core problem: The obstacle is not whether the sub-brand sells fashionable products. The obstacle is the old parent-brand perception.

Wrong path: Directly proving "we are fashionable" makes the brand sound defensive.

Transferable rule: When the obstacle is old perception, do not argue against it head-on. Create evidence that users can feel the brand is changing.

Use when: brand transformation, old-brand rejuvenation, parent-brand shadow, category stretch.

Sharp line: Do not prove "I am X"; let users feel "I am becoming X in a believable way."

## 2. Functional Product Needs A New Habit

Type: `整合营销`

Scenario: A household-care product asks users to add one extra step to an existing routine.

Core problem: Users do not yet understand why this extra behavior is necessary.

Wrong path: Frightening users with risk may educate the category but push them toward easier substitutes.

Transferable rule: Category education should first teach a concrete behavior, then prove why the behavior is worth it.

Use when: new category education, functional product communication, habit building.

Sharp line: First make the user do one more step; then make the step feel necessary.

## 3. Commodity Product Needs Memory

Type: `创意/内容`

Scenario: A highly similar food product has many quality standards, but consumers see only a generic product.

Core problem: The difference exists in production standards, not in consumer memory.

Wrong path: Listing quality, safety, and standards like every competitor.

Transferable rule: For commodity categories, create a cognitive contrast before explaining standards.

Use when: homogeneous categories, product quality hard to perceive, many selling points but no memory point.

Sharp line: A standard must become a story, symbol, or phrase before users remember it.

## 4. Local-Life Platform Needs A Role Beyond Discounts

Type: `策略/品牌`

Scenario: A service platform wants to avoid being seen only as a coupon or low-price tool.

Core problem: Discounts create use, but not necessarily brand role or long-term preference.

Wrong path: Adding emotional copy on top of a discount mechanism.

Transferable rule: Platform brands need a role in users' lives, not only a transaction benefit.

Use when: platform positioning, local services, utility-to-brand upgrade.

Sharp line: The platform should not only make things cheaper; it should make a kind of life easier to choose.

## 5. Youth Node Campaign Avoids Preaching

Type: `传播/公关`

Scenario: A brand wants to speak to young people during a life milestone.

Core problem: The moment is emotionally loaded, so brands easily become preachy or sentimental.

Wrong path: Giving grand life advice or generic encouragement.

Transferable rule: For young audiences, a small concrete reminder can be more powerful than a big blessing.

Use when: graduation, first job, moving city, youth milestone, brand companionship.

Sharp line: Do not stand above the user and teach life; stand beside them and notice one real detail.

## 6. Festival Campaign Needs A New Role

Type: `创意/内容`

Scenario: A brand wants to make a festival film around a relationship everyone already praises.

Core problem: The standard emotion is already overused.

Wrong path: Repeating that family, love, or gratitude is important.

Transferable rule: Festival communication needs a new role or angle, not a louder version of the same emotion.

Use when: Mother's Day, Father's Day, Mid-Autumn, Chinese New Year, relationship themes.

Sharp line: Do not praise the role again; reveal something about the role that people have not named.

## 7. Scene Binding For A Content Platform

Type: `整合营销`

Scenario: A content platform wants users to associate it with a daily usage scene.

Core problem: "We have rich content" does not create a usage habit.

Wrong path: Talking broadly about content variety or user emotion.

Transferable rule: Scene binding needs repeated role, concrete trigger, and a fixed phrase or action.

Use when: content platforms, habit formation, usage frequency, daily scene campaigns.

Sharp line: Do not say "we have many things"; own one moment when users should think of you.

## 8. Expert Brand Needs Attitude, Not Only Proof

Type: `策略/品牌`

Scenario: A functional brand wants to own an expert position in a crowded benefit category.

Core problem: Everyone can claim expertise, so proof alone may feel cold or interchangeable.

Wrong path: Stacking data, certifications, or technical claims without a user-facing attitude.

Transferable rule: Expert positioning becomes stronger when expertise enables a more confident user behavior.

Use when: functional brands, expert mind, premium benefit, technical categories.

Sharp line: Expertise should not only say "we work hard"; it should let users act with more confidence.

## 9. Big Promotion Needs A Symbol

Type: `整合营销`

Scenario: A retail brand runs a seasonal promotion with strong price benefits.

Core problem: Users are surrounded by discounts and may remember only that everyone is cheap.

Wrong path: Repeating price, subsidy, and limited-time urgency.

Transferable rule: Promotion should turn the price promise into a repeatable asset: a phrase, symbol, rule, or ritual.

Use when: shopping festival, low-price mind, retail campaign, promotion-to-brand asset.

Sharp line: Promotion is not only making users know you are cheap once; it is making them remember why you are the place for that value.

## 10. New Brand Uses Discovery, Not Maturity

Type: `创意/内容`

Scenario: A new consumer brand wants to be noticed in a crowded category.

Core problem: It lacks mature trust assets, but may have freshness, specificity, and surprise.

Wrong path: Communicating like a large mature brand: quality, technology, scale, guarantee.

Transferable rule: New brands can win first attention by making users feel they discovered something oddly specific and useful.

Use when: new brand, new product, niche category, discovery content.

Sharp line: A new brand does not always need to look big first; it can first make people think, "Someone finally made this."

## 11. IP Collaboration Needs A Sales Bridge

Type: `整合营销`

Scenario: A product launch borrows a popular character, meme, or cultural symbol.

Core problem: The IP creates attention, but the proposal does not explain why it helps this product sell through this channel.

Wrong path: Assuming popularity equals conversion.

Transferable rule: Evaluate IP content and product/channel conversion separately, then build the bridge.

Use when: IP collaboration, meme marketing, new product launch, channel-exclusive campaign.

Sharp line: Attention is not a sales path until it explains why this product, here, now.

## 12. Public-Value Campaign Needs Participation

Type: `传播/公关`

Scenario: A brand or platform wants to express a social value or public attitude.

Core problem: The value is agreeable, but users have no concrete behavior and media have no new proof.

Wrong path: Writing a manifesto and calling it a campaign.

Transferable rule: Public value becomes communication only when it becomes an action, proof, report, or participation mechanism.

Use when: CSR, public issue, platform value, brand attitude, reputation campaign.

Sharp line: A public statement is not a public campaign until someone can do something with it.
