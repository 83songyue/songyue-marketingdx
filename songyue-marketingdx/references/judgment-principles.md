# Judgment Principles

These public principles are distilled for L1.5 marketing diagnosis. They are written as observable checks, not slogans. Use them to make the diagnosis sharper before reaching for case examples.

## Problem And Brief

1. A proposal must define the real business problem, not only repeat the communication task.
2. A client Brief gives constraints and resources; it does not automatically prove the proposal is good.
3. If no Brief is provided, diagnose only the submitted text and label missing assumptions.
4. The sharper diagnosis often begins by rewriting the problem in one sentence.
5. Do not let a beautiful campaign idea hide a wrong problem definition.

## Audience And Scenario

6. Audience labels are weak until they are placed in a concrete scene.
7. A useful insight names who is stuck, where they are stuck, and why they would act.
8. Trend language is not insight unless it explains a specific choice or behavior.
9. Emotional value must describe a change of emotional state, not only a warm tone.
10. If the same insight can be pasted into any category, it is not yet sharp.

## Proposition And Brand Role

11. A proposition should contain a point of view, not only a positive adjective.
12. The brand should be part of the problem or the solution, not a logo at the end.
13. Delete-brand test: if the idea still works unchanged after removing the brand, brand relevance is weak.
14. Brand relevance does not require absolute uniqueness; it requires that this brand doing it feels more natural than a random competitor.
15. Existing brand assets should be translated, not discarded, when a brand wants to upgrade.

## Creative And Memory

16. A creative idea needs a repeatable handle: a phrase, action, symbol, scene, contrast, or event.
17. A theme is not a creative idea until people can retell it.
18. A good idea often turns an abstract attitude into a concrete behavior.
19. Humor, emotion, or IP attention must still connect back to the product, brand role, or user action.
20. If the audience remembers only the media format, not the brand idea, the creative memory is weak.

## Communication And Action

21. A channel list is not a communication strategy.
22. Communication momentum needs a reason for someone to talk, join, share, report, or imitate.
23. One main action is usually stronger than many unrelated touchpoints.
24. Different platforms can use different formats, but they need one shared story, role, or asset.

## Growth And Evidence

25. Growth does not always mean immediate sales, but it must name the next user behavior or asset that should change.
26. Coupons, QR codes, UTM links, and KPI dashboards are measurement or conversion tools; they do not by themselves prove growth logic.
27. When several weaknesses exist, choose the one that most damages the proposal's persuasiveness and make the revision direction revolve around it.
