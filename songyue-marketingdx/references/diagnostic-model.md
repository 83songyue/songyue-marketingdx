# Public L1.5 Diagnostic Model

Use this reference to make the public skill judge in the same direction as the online L1.5 experience. It is a public working model: do not add private root-cause routes, production prompts, customer mappings, or hidden calibration material.

## Six Dimensions

| Dimension | Judge this question |
| --- | --- |
| `洞察锐度` | Does the plan find a concrete human conflict in a concrete decision or usage moment, rather than a trend, label, or generic emotion? |
| `创意记忆` | Is there a phrase, action, symbol, scene, contrast, or event name that people can retell? |
| `品牌关联` | Does the direction grow from a real brand, product, service, channel, or relationship support point, rather than a brand sign-off? |
| `传播势能` | Is there a reason to discuss, participate, report, repeat, or extend the idea, rather than a media list? |
| `逻辑通顺` | Can Brief, insight, brand opportunity, concept, and actions be naturally derived one from another? |
| `增长有效` | Can the plan connect a target person's behavior change to a business, relationship, channel, or brand-asset result? |

Do not replace these with a general consulting checklist such as business problem, audience, channel, or feasibility. Those are evidence inputs to the six dimensions, not the dimensions themselves.

## Type Applicability

| Proposal type | Evaluate by default | Mark `本方案不评` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `整合营销` | 洞察锐度、创意记忆、品牌关联、传播势能、逻辑通顺、增长有效 | None |
| `策略/品牌` | 洞察锐度、品牌关联、逻辑通顺、增长有效 | 创意记忆、传播势能 |
| `创意/内容` | 洞察锐度、创意记忆、品牌关联、逻辑通顺 | 传播势能、增长有效 |
| `传播/公关` | 洞察锐度、创意记忆、传播势能、逻辑通顺 | 品牌关联、增长有效 |

Only add a normally non-applicable dimension when the Brief explicitly assigns that result to this proposal. Quote that Brief task in the response. A plan merely mentioning an extra item is not sufficient reason to expand the evaluation.

## Evidence-First Quality Judgment

Do not calculate a `/60` total or give an overall grade. For every applicable dimension, make a visible quality judgment using only `2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10`, with one to three evidence points from the submitted material.

| Quality mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `2` | A necessary part is missing and the plan cannot stand up without it. |
| `4` | It is mentioned but generic, weak, replaceable, or disconnected from the rest. |
| `6` | It is structurally complete and safe, but ordinary and not proposal-strengthening. |
| `8` | It is specific and differentiated enough to materially strengthen persuasion. |
| `10` | This dimension is itself the plan's memorable, expandable core strength. |

Choose one `当前最该加分` dimension. It normally comes from the lowest or next-lowest applicable judgment, but can be a nearby score when it is the bottleneck that most affects the whole proposal. Explain that choice with proposal evidence; never manufacture score gaps for visual neatness.

### Basic Quality Ceilings

- Without a real conflict or decision tension, `洞察锐度` cannot exceed `6`.
- With only a theme or slogan and no symbol, action, scene, content mechanism, or expression system, `创意记忆` cannot exceed `6`.
- With a logo/end card or post-hoc selling point only, `品牌关联` cannot exceed `4`; if it relates but remains easily replaceable, it cannot exceed `6`.
- With only media, KOL, activity, or channel lists, `传播势能` cannot exceed `4`.
- When the stages are present but the key inference is not inevitable, `逻辑通顺` cannot exceed `6`.
- With a business target but no `target person -> behavior change -> result` causal chain, `增长有效` cannot exceed `6`.
- QR codes, coupons, tracking links, countdowns, and KPIs are handoff or measurement details. They cannot independently raise `增长有效` above `6`.

### Dimension Anchors

| Dimension | `2` | `4` | `6` | `8` | `10` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 洞察锐度 | No relevant insight | Trend, label, or broad emotion | Relevant behavior, but common tension | Specific conflict that can derive an idea | The brand role and idea almost follow naturally |
| 创意记忆 | No core expression | Generic theme or disconnected execution | Complete concept, hard to retell | Clear memory anchor | Naturally becomes a topic, action, or visual symbol |
| 品牌关联 | Brand role absent or contradictory | End-card/signature only | Related but easily replaced | Grows from a real brand support point | Brand/product/service is why the idea works |
| 传播势能 | No public expression or main action | Resource or channel list only | Retellable topic/event, ordinary amplification | Clear main action plus at least two amplification forces | Naturally repeatable, high-amplification, durable asset |
| 逻辑通顺 | Premise and conclusion conflict | Stages exist but visibly break | Direction understandable, key bridge weak | Chain holds up to questioning | Problem to idea to action derives naturally |
| 增长有效 | No target or behavioral result | Target and action disconnect | Behavior/result link exists but is loose | Clear people-behavior-result causality | Result can be sustained as business, relationship, channel, or brand asset |

For `品牌关联`, do not demand that no competitor could ever run the idea. The useful standard is whether this brand running it feels more natural, better evidenced, or more meaningful.

For `传播势能`, do not equate amplification with UGC. A public issue, reportable proof, authoritative response, industry debate, or event can be valid amplification.

## Brief Resource Boundary

- Treat the Brief as context, not proof of execution quality.
- List only Brief-verifiable IP, talent, theme, holiday, product claim, platform, or resource as `Brief 已给资源`.
- Those givens do not count as plan capability by themselves and earn no insight, creative, or brand points. Judge the proposal only on what it newly adds: a human conflict, brand role, central concept, expression system, or action mechanism.
- Do not cite a client-given resource as the sole evidence for a high-quality dimension.
- If no Brief is provided, write: `未提供 Brief，以下判断只基于方案文本。`
- Never invent budget, capacity, media resource, brand history, sales performance, audience data, product capability, or customer requirement. When a proposed improvement depends on an unprovided capability, write `若品牌可提供...` or `需客户确认...`.

## Long-Document Protocol

1. Read any `文本覆盖` note first. Material not included in an excerpt is unknown, not absent from the original proposal.
2. Map a long document by heading, page, or natural section.
3. Capture one to three evidence points per applicable dimension before judging.
4. Say `当前读取片段未见` rather than claiming an unshown page, image, video, or full proposal has no such content.
5. If the material cannot be read in one pass, request the next section or the target pages before making a whole-plan claim.

## Public Boundary

Stay at public L1.5: identify the proposal type, evidence-supported quality gaps, one primary opportunity, and a specific revision direction. Do not reveal private root-cause chains, expert correction libraries, risk rules, real case mappings, or production implementation details.
